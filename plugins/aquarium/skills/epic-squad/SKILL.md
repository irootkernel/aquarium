## Accept, integrate and close out

The Coordinator accepts the exact current composition only when all due criteria,
checks, assessments and material disagreements are resolved. Keep Task-level
contribution passage separate from whole-Epic passage. The Coordinator alone
stages and invokes required commit tooling under the actual
`aquarium:epic-squad` owner and its applicable unconsumed grant. Missing Squad
commit integration blocks that effect; a Handler handoff is not a substitute.

Keep useful same-Task context through corrections and post-commit inspection.
Start a new Task team only after the preceding Task's required commit and native
finalization are verified. Recover a successful commit without replaying it.
Final integration independently assesses the assembled original Epic with EE
and at least two fresh non-authoring Task Operators, alongside the Coordinator's
product review. Internal validation uses Squad's own owner and does not invoke
the cold `epic-validator` implicitly.

Prepare closeout only after current integration evidence is complete. Ask for
actual overall user verification and acceptance once, then consume only the
matching conditional closeout grant. Preserve required functional evidence,
pending external handoffs and source-versus-installed limits. Report actual
commits, native finalization, checks, unverified routes and remaining work.
