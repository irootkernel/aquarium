# Haepari Consumer Contract

Aquarium's host adapter (`make test-vm-host`) submits sealed suites with fixed profiles and recipe-owned guest assertions and cleanup. Its source repository owns `tests/vm/README.md`, the scenario runbooks under `tests/vm/recipes/scenarios/`, and `docs/ops/haepari-vm-verification.md`. TASK-089 supplies the uninterrupted `release-acceptance` transaction; TASK-090 qualifies the installed policy cutover. The existing manual release path stays authoritative until that acceptance. Other repositories use their declared adapters and scenario owners.

The [tool catalog](tool-catalog.md#haepari) owns support and setup policy. The release-matched [v0.1.0 sources](https://github.com/irootkernel/haepari/tree/v0.1.0) own native behavior; this reference owns Aquarium's consumer boundary.

## Scope

Managed automatic and manual Jobs on `local` and `utm-macos` resources, and advisory named locks, per the upstream [CLI and job](https://github.com/irootkernel/haepari/blob/v0.1.0/docs/contracts/cli-and-jobs.md), [resource and runner](https://github.com/irootkernel/haepari/blob/v0.1.0/docs/contracts/resource-and-runner.md), [lock](https://github.com/irootkernel/haepari/blob/v0.1.0/docs/contracts/locks.md), [configuration](https://github.com/irootkernel/haepari/blob/v0.1.0/docs/contracts/configuration.md), [managed transition](https://github.com/irootkernel/haepari/blob/v0.1.0/docs/contracts/managed-transitions.md) and [artifact and profile](https://github.com/irootkernel/haepari/blob/v0.1.0/docs/contracts/artifact-and-profile.md) contracts. The frozen native schemas are the ones the exact release's `help` reports: the `haepari.job` and `haepari.result` v1/v2 families, `haepari.resource` v1/v2, and `haepari.manual-result/v1`. Admission supports only the `development` evidence class; consumer provenance carries the committed source and release/archive identities, and no other evidence class is required or assumed. Mulgae `>=v0.1.27` natively owns capacity-one `mulgae-review` (`advisory-lock/v1`) acquisition, renewal, `HAEPARI_LOCK_GRANT` borrower validation, and terminal release/drain in the selected `HAEPARI_HOME` ([mulgae-review-contract.md](mulgae-review-contract.md)); Aquarium adds no outer review-lock wrapper, and guest live-review checks inside a held `utm-macos` Job use an isolated guest controller/root without reacquiring the parent VM resource. Excluded: external leases and linked continuation ([deferred upstream](https://github.com/irootkernel/haepari/blob/v0.1.0/docs/contracts/external-leases.md)), any VM power operation (Haepari never starts, stops, clones or resets the VM), and host installation authority, which stays with setup under its own approvals.

## Submission

Before the first submission, persist in a private mode-0700 run directory the request key, the exact spec bytes, and the selected installation, resource, and sealed-input identities: controller root, resource ID, and the spec's input inventory digest, plus the installation ID once it is known. Persist the Job ID when it is returned or recovered. Submit once. A lost or uncertain admission reply is recovered by resending the identical key and spec, never a replacement key. Preserve `error.code` and `error.retry`: exit 6 covers both transport and storage uncertainty and does not by itself authorize replay. Act on the native hint: `retry_same_key` resends the identical request, `reconcile` inspects or reconciles the same key, `none` stops.

Aquarium's adapter builds the plugin from the selected committed source in a clean disposable clone through `make aquarium-dev-build`. The producer returns `aquarium-dev-artifact-manifest/v1` with `git_sha`, `development_version`, and `sha256`; the bundle records its own sealed tree digest separately. Caller and nested-build interpreter selection follows the source repository's `TESTING.md` and VM guide. A dirty source overlay cannot be represented as a committed plugin artifact.

## Observation and results

Observe through native blocking `wait`, `events`, and `logs`; no wrapper adds a status-polling loop. Terminal mapping: exit 0 PASS, 1 FAIL, 3 INCOMPLETE, 4 CANCELLED. A caller wait timeout (exit 5) reports PENDING and never cancels the Job; uncertainty (exit 6) reports UNKNOWN and follows the native `error.code` and `error.retry`. Exit 2 remains a classified refusal or error, not a synthetic terminal verdict.

Aquarium's wrapper accepts `AQUARIUM_VM_WAIT_TIMEOUT` as integer seconds (default `900`) and `AQUARIUM_VM_WAIT_ROUNDS` as an integer (default `480`). It persists observations and cancellation state in `RUN_DIR/run.json` and emits JSON-line reports on stdout. Capture its process exit separately: GNU Make maps a failed recipe to exit 2. Keep workload outcome, evidence completeness, Haepari-owned cleanup, consumer cleanup, and native final verdict separate; missing observations or failed consumer cleanup prevent acceptance.

## Collection ordering

Stage case evidence; finish consumer cleanup and stage its verified inventory; then end the workload, by automatic exit or `manual complete`; native collection and Haepari-owned cleanup follow; export collected results last. Export is never a prerequisite for consumer cleanup.

## Release transaction

Release acceptance requires the sealed official Podway v0.2.17 input and selects `podway-candidate`. It preserves original baseline fixtures and uses fresh generation-2 candidate workspaces. Successful consumer cleanup retains the tested Aquarium candidate, Podway CLI, paired skill and selected candidate status runtime in the dedicated test VM, verified by the final inventory. Other suites keep their declared restoration contracts. One release-acceptance Job holds the VM continuously from the first baseline observation through candidate installation, scenarios, evidence collection, and owned cleanup. TASK-089 ships that Job as the `release-acceptance` suite: the machine preparation runs inside `prepare_seconds`, every later phase is an operator step under the runbook invoked from `<workspace>/harness` through `/bin/sh tests/vm/recipes/guest_entry.sh` with the saved spec's `workload.environment` and `HAEPARI_PARENT_CONTEXT` read from `../operator-parent-context.json` (mode `0600`), the approval-based status-runtime update runs after candidate installation and before candidate repository setups, the EPIC-024 startup smokes run through the unchanged guest gate, and evidence travels in fixed-name per-case archives with validating content manifests, all declared as required outputs fixed before admission. TASK-088's `manual-hold` suite declares two manual scenarios (`fixture-check` and `discovery-check`) and five required outputs (`results/manual-hold/{result.json,inventories.json,smoke.json,consumer-cleanup.json,post-cleanup-inventory.json}`), completed via `haepari manual complete <job-id> --input <file> --expected-revision <n> --json`, and carries the manual-path demonstrations, including same-`RUN_DIR`/same-Job reconnect with receipt-verified export reuse. Independent suites may use separate Jobs only when each checks and establishes its own starting state. `SUITE=all` selects all declared suites alphabetically, using each suite's first declared mode rather than filtering by `MODE`; a nonzero result skips later suites. Select suites explicitly for a passing verification sequence, retaining deliberate failure and unobserved cases separately.

## Release-role and producer adapter handoff

EPIC-031's release roles and EPIC-032/EPIC-033's producer packets use this durable contract without the EPIC-019 dossier:

- **Pre-submission records.** Before the first submission, persist the request key, the exact spec bytes, the controller identity (the explicit `HAEPARI_HOME` root with its `installation_id`), the resource identity (registered id, kind, configuration revision), the committed candidate/source SHA, the sealed artifact digest, and the starting/target profile digests, exactly as the Submission section requires.
- **Returned records.** The Job ID and its `job_revision` do not exist before admission; retain each when it is returned or recovered, and never treat a missing Job ID as a pre-submission failure.
- **Original observations.** Retain the native envelopes (`wait`, `events`, `inspect` receipts, the export's `manifest.json` and `result.json`) and original candidate-bound results. Keep workload outcome, evidence completeness, Haepari-owned cleanup, consumer cleanup and the final verdict separate. Consumer cleanup uses the result and inventory outputs declared by the producer's suite; `manual-hold` declares `post-cleanup-inventory.json`, while `release-acceptance` declares `inventory-post-cleanup.json`. These suite artifacts are distinct from the native export-root result and manifest. A successful command with missing required observations or failed consumer cleanup cannot establish acceptance, and no wrapper synthesizes PASS. Preserve the native `error.code` and `error.retry` distinctions.
- **Wait and recovery.** Observe through native blocking `wait`: "each `wait` call accepts at most a 24-hour timeout. A caller wait deadline returns exit 5 while the Job continues" (verified against the installed v0.1.0 binary and the tagged `cli-and-jobs.md`). A caller timeout reports PENDING without cancelling; transport or storage uncertainty reports UNKNOWN and reconciles the same request key; an uncertain admission reply replays the identical key and spec (a changed payload is `CONFLICT`); never generate a replacement key; unknown execution follows native reconciliation; no status-polling loop.
- **Disposition and cleanup.** Haepari owns collection and Job cleanup after `manual complete` or automatic workload exit; the recipe owns consumer cleanup, verified with its inventory before completion. A wait timeout is not cancellation; cancellation preserves actual native outcomes and unfinished consumer cleanup is recorded, never claimed clean.
- **Release startup.** Aquarium's managed release acceptance adds one bounded installed `release-handler` plan-readiness case alongside Task/Epic startup, stopping before settlement commit, pre-QA gates, QA or publication. Its source-owned runbook is `tests/vm/recipes/scenarios/release-acceptance.md`; the separate `release-orchestration` development suite owns full feature scenarios. Preserve the unchanged readiness probe (`doctor --resource`) and queue rule (`(jobs ahead + 1) × 26100`, ceiling 86400). Prepared scenarios do not establish installed results.

## Final distribution identity

The checks the release workflow and TASK-090 execute, defined here:

- **Durable owner.** This section, pointed to from the tool integrations specification.
- **Distribution inputs.** The official distribution payload is the release tag's marketplace payload or the published archive carrying the release version in its plugin manifest. A `make aquarium-dev-build` output never qualifies, because the producer always rewrites the manifest to `<version>-dev.<sha12>`, which cannot satisfy a released-version predicate. Each payload binds to its own source commit, manifest version and digests.
- **Bindings.** Against the tested candidate, the expected and declared difference is the manifest version stamp alone; the check requires every other payload byte and both skill trees (`aquarium-skill-tree-sha256/v1`) to equal the tested candidate's sealed inputs, or alternatively binds the release commit's source tag to the tested candidate SHA.
- **Renewal condition.** Any difference beyond that declared stamp, in archive, source, binary or skill-tree bytes, voids prior installed observations for those bytes and requires renewed installed verification before release. The repository's release-metadata and publication order (`AGENTS.md` Release Policy) is unchanged, and a development-integration result is never presented as official-distribution evidence.

## Profiles and budgets

Consumer profiles are immutable test inputs pinning exact binary, controller, skill, rules, and effective configuration identities inside the tool-catalog support policy; they add no global registry or account-wide exception. Resolve any default or latest alias before admission, retain the exact profile digest, and never resolve latest while an admitted Job executes. Queue waiting must not let later worktree changes alter the admitted candidate: sealed input bytes are the candidate identity. Choose phase budgets, including enough manual time for the cases, evidence staging, and consumer cleanup, before admission; reconnection never extends a deadline.

## Shared-VM occupancy after cutover

After TASK-090's accepted policy cutover, Aquarium-owned installation-dependent checks targeting the registered shared VM use the accepted managed adapter for occupancy, acquired once at the outer host boundary. Guest entrypoints and nested handler fixtures run inside the held Job without recursively acquiring its resource. Participating callers must select the same native installation and resource; Haepari does not exclude direct access that bypasses it.

## Workflow verification routing

Release, Task, Epic, Squad, and Validation workflows select due installation-dependent checks during planning under the target repository's verification environment policy. When that policy requires shared-VM execution, the workflow routes those checks through the repository's declared adapter, immutable profiles, and registered shared resource under explicit VM-test authority. Local-only checks, repositories without a VM requirement, and offline `release-qa` keep their existing native contracts without a new VM gate. Participating tool repositories supply their supported commands, tests, and paired skills; verification alone requires no new Haepari dependency, adapter, controller, or instruction file.

One outer Job holds shared VM occupancy across preparation, guest execution, evidence staging, and cleanup. Guest entrypoints, tool checks, and nested fixtures run inside that allocation without reacquiring the parent resource. A single suitable Job or verified original result may cover one or several Tasks when its exact inputs, coverage, and environment apply; no per-tool controller, resource, scenario framework, or one-Job-per-Task rule applies. Callers reconnect to the original Job across wait timeouts (exit 5 `PENDING`) and transport or storage uncertainty (exit 6 `UNKNOWN`) using the persisted request key, spec bytes, and `job_id`. Before redispatching work or replacing a mutating Executor, reconcile that Job and settle the Executor's modifying activity. Replacing only its caller or observer does not require terminating the active Job.

Outer Job observation, cancellation, and cleanup stay independent of the tool or candidate under test, which never owns the outer VM resource. If an inner command or candidate fails, keep outer result collection independent and finish recipe-owned consumer cleanup with its verified post-cleanup inventory before automatic workload exit or `manual complete`. Native collection, Haepari-owned Job cleanup, and export follow in that order. At final Epic validation, assembled Squad integration, or cold validation, reuse completed Task checks and original results whose inputs, coverage, dependencies, and environment still apply, and rerun only missing or affected behavior and required current-target gates. Keep original source, binary/package, skill, and profile/Job identities, prior-state restoration, actual failures, manual gaps, and direct-versus-managed limits distinct: an explicitly authorized direct guest run stays direct evidence and never establishes a managed Haepari Job `PASS`.
