# Validation orchestration

Validation version `33` uses ordinary Actions and the [v2 contract](ordinary-agent-orchestration.md).
An independent cold Judge reads original Epic criteria and current artifacts;
internal validation retains its logical Epic Judge when supported. Use available
public handles, actual evidence and authority without qualification, private
history or configuration-digest admission. Admitted formal sessions retain their
original [v1 contract](formal-agent-orchestration-v1.md) and genuine receipts.

## Audit and correction checkpoints

`audit` and `re-audit` are direct report-only Judge assessments. Before each
dispatch, the Primary or an authorized Worker executes the due repository checks
and supplies their actual results and current candidate evidence. The Judge reads
that evidence without running checks. The Primary records the aggregate audit and
check result. Their checkpoint Decisions require current
`judge-applicability` and passing `judge-checkpoint-result` before the existing
`decide-gaps` or `decide-re-audit` router. The checkpoint checks a complete,
faithful audit within its admitted full or frozen scope. Its report preserves
actual original Epic unmet and unverified rows, verdict, gap identities, counts
and authority. A faithful negative audit can pass this checkpoint and route its
real blockers, confirmation gaps or Low findings without claiming Epic success.

Incomplete audit evidence enters its local followup record and effect-authority
Decision. The baseline and prepared pass supply authority before provider
records exist. Inapplicable reports return to their current Judge; missing or
stale candidate evidence cannot stand in for reassessment. Negative reports never restore
a consumed correction envelope or technical-review ordinal.

`remediate` implements one admitted correction group and authorized checks.
Its Worker records candidate bytes and source findings, without accepting,
staging or committing. `judge-remediation` reads every original Epic row and retains its full outcome
verdict and actual unmet/unverified counts. Its separate `judge-group-result`
checks actual repair of this exact group, required checks and applicable parent
constraints. A faithful report alone cannot establish group repair. A current
group result may pass while other original Epic gaps remain negative; their
rows remain unchanged for final assessment. After a
current pass, the Primary at `settle-remediation-group` performs exact delegated
group acceptance and the authorized `$aquarium:task-commit` handoff, then checks
actual post-hook commit bytes, identity, owner attribution and residue.
`group-settlement-result` must pass before `decide-remediation-groups` admits
another serialized group or the original post-remediation audit scope.

The settlement summary carries every earlier verified group from retained records
and actual commit evidence. Corrections retain original task or Epic ownership,
dependency order and distinct unconsumed group grants within the same Validation
session. No per-group Goal session, concurrent correction set, implicit Worker
commit or per-group provider review applies. Each actual diff gets its own
bounded commit handoff; no-change results never create an empty commit. The
whole-Epic review of corrected committed bytes remains pending.

## Technical evidence and final outcome

`final-review` delivers the selected operation's complete native evidence.
Its existing serial route, operation, ordinal, provenance, backend, finding and
operational-readiness Decisions remain separate. Only their admitted passed
route reaches `judge-validation-evidence`, then its Primary semantic Decision
and the existing completion, required-evidence, blocker and Low routing. Its
current `judge-checkpoint-result` assesses actual evidence-phase obligations and
already-due parent constraints while preserving every original global row, verdict
and unmet/unverified count. Faithful negative provider evidence reaches those
original routers; report completeness alone cannot prove required corrections.

A Judge correction returns through the existing review-mode classification and
an additional exact effect-authority check. Evidence collection uses its local
authority check and reconciles an existing selected operation before dispatch.
Preserve the three work-unit assessments and one remediation confirmation,
original frozen scope and actual one-shot later direction. Neither a Judge
report nor Low settlement consumes a provider ordinal. Audit and provider
finding identities remain separate; zero provider findings cannot erase an
applicable audit Low obligation.

The final Primary assembly `prepare-validation-assessment` and `judge-validation`
consume every original criterion, current full or frozen audit, provider/Judge
report, verified correction commit, applicable manual result and local composition.
Preserve the reviewed basis, permitted delta, exact after-target, current local
checks and criterion evidence; the earlier review predates the local delta.
A complete existing final report may be reused only with current exact coverage,
candidate, requirements and checks; assembly alone causes no new Judge dispatch.
Otherwise obtain fresh affected assessment before `assess-goal`. Achievement requires its exact
`achieved` outcome, pass verdict, zero mandatory unmet/unverified rows and fresh
original native criterion assessments. Passing members cannot erase a failed
integration or an unsupported manual claim.

## Stopped validation

A stop, incomplete audit, confirmation-incomplete or provider-incomplete result
binds `record-stopped-goal-boundary`. Baseline and this exact boundary are
required; legitimately bypassed audit, re-audit, provider/Judge, correction and
Low records remain optional. Supply every available record, including actual
direction and route-stop evidence, in the complete final assignment.

Both core branches reach stopped Primary evidence assembly and an applicable
independent stopped Judge assessment. `needs-rework` first records bounded
`record-stopped-core-gaps`; reconciliation preserves unresolved gaps and cannot
reopen ordinary work, provider admission, normal assessment or achievement.
Inapplicable stopped reports return to that stopped Judge. Only actual renewed
user direction at stopped closeout can re-enter ordinary work.

Cold validation records actual user acceptance with `acceptance-source=user`.
Internal Validation may use its applicable Epic delegation; final overall
Master acceptance remains required for later Epic closeout. Preserve exact
accepted bytes and the existing separate commit, publication and installation
boundaries. A clean no-change validation records its supported no-change result
without a validation-record or empty commit.

After admitted review evidence, apply the [local-settlement contract](finding-disposition.md#decide-whether-a-correction-needs-another-review) before provider-rework authority. Preserve source negative rows, verify the exact correction and current criteria, then supply the complete composition to the required final Judge. `confirm-local-corrections-settled` checks pending repairs before ordinary Low completion. Native backend failures and missing reviewer coverage retain their existing gates.
